/ FEATURES

Built for real stores.
Designed for real people.

⬡

Real-Time Object Detection

YOLOv7-tiny runs entirely on-device via SnapML, identifying grocery items in your field of view with sub-200ms latency. No cloud. No lag.

YOLOv7-tiny SnapML <200ms
☑

In-View Checklist

Your shopping list overlays directly in the AR view. Items auto-check when detected by the camera.

◎

Hands-Free

No phone needed while shopping. Control your list via the Spectacles touchpad or companion app before you enter the store.

⟁

Indoor Navigation Coming 195B

⊕

Focus Mode Coming 195B

/ HOW IT WORKS

From list to cart
in three steps.

01

Build Your List

Open the AisleVision companion app and type or voice-dictate your grocery list. Items sync to your Spectacles over WiFi.

02

Put On Your Spectacles

The AisleVision lens launches automatically. The ML model begins detecting grocery items the moment you look at the shelves.

03

Shop and Check Off

Recognized items are highlighted and checked off your list in real time. When the list is clear, you're done.
